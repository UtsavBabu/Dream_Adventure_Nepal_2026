## 1. Langtang Valley Trek (8 Days)

**Duration:** 8 Days (6 days trekking, 2 days in Kathmandu)
**Region:** Langtang
**Max Altitude:** 4,800 m / 15,655 ft (Kyanjin Ri)
**Grade:** Moderate
**Start:** Syabrubesi | **End:** Kathmandu
**Transport:** Public Bus

### Price
- **Base price:** Not explicitly listed (Porter cost: USD 184 optional)
- **Optional Porter (8 days):** USD 184
- **Private Jeep:** USD 240
- **Sleeping bag/down jacket rental:** available at office

### Highlights
- No need to book a plane (7-8 hr drive from Kathmandu)
- Trek through Langtang Valley, the 'Valley of Glaciers'
- Climb Tserko Ri (4,985m) for stunning scenic views
- Views of Langtang Lirung (7,245m), Langtang Ri, Langsisa, Ganjala Peak
- Experience Tibetan Buddhist culture firsthand
- Walk through spectacular rhododendron forests
- Visit Kyanjin Gompa and Langtang Gompa
- One of the lesser-known treks in Nepal

### Full Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Kathmandu to Syabrubesi (Drive)**
- Drive time: 7-8 hours
- Distance: 122 km / 75 miles
- Altitude: 1,500 m / 4,790 ft
- Overnight: Syabrubesi
- Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
- Bus journey from Kathmandu along the Trisuli River, passing Trisuli Bazaar with views of Manaslu Massif, Ganesh, Jungal Himal, and Langtang

**Day 2: Trek from Syabrubesi to Sherpa Gaun**
- Trek time: 7 hours
- Distance: 10 km / 6.2 miles
- Altitude: 2,563 m / 8,409 ft
- Overnight: Sherpa Gaun
- Walk through beautiful forest with Langtang Lirung backdrop. Pass Ghora Tabela with green meadows

**Day 3: Sherpa Gaun to Langtang Village**
- Trek time: 8 hours
- Distance: 16 km / 9.9 miles
- Altitude: 3,430 m / 11,255 ft
- Overnight: Langtang Village
- Pass through forest to Ghoretable (horse stables). Langtang Village rebuilt after 2015 earthquake

**Day 4: Langtang Village to Kyanjin Gompa**
- Trek time: 3 hours
- Distance: 7 km / 4.3 miles
- Altitude: 3,870 m / 12,696 ft
- Overnight: Kyanjin Gompa
- Shorter route lined with Buddhist artifacts: chortens, prayer flags, mani stones

**Day 5: Kyanjin Gompa to Tserko Ri to Kyanjin Gompa**
- Trek time: 8-9 hours
- Distance: 16 km / 10 miles
- Altitude: 4,985 m / 16,354 ft (Tserko Ri)
- Overnight: Kyanjin Gompa
- Hike to Tsergo Ri for views of 20+ peaks, Langtang Valley, and frozen lakes

**Day 6: Kyanjin Gompa to Lama Hotel**
- Trek time: 6 hours
- Distance: 14.5 km / 9 miles
- Altitude: 2,470 m / 8,100 ft
- Overnight: Lama Hotel
- Descend from alpine zone through forests of pine, oak, rhododendron

**Day 7: Lama Hotel to Syabrubesi**
- Trek time: 6-7 hours
- Distance: 11.3 km / 7 miles
- Altitude: 1,550 m / 5,085 ft
- Overnight: Syabrubesi
- Final day on trail through rhododendron, oak, bamboo forests and waterfalls

**Day 8: Syabrubesi to Kathmandu (Drive)**
- Drive time: 7-9 hours
- Overnight: Kathmandu
- Scenic drive through Rasuwa and Nuwakot hills

### Includes
- 7 nights teahouse accommodation
- Guide for 8 days
- Kathmandu-Syabrubesi-Kathmandu local bus
- Langtang National Park permit
- 8 breakfasts, 8 lunches, 7 dinners on trek

### Excludes
- Porter (optional USD 184)
- Private Jeep (optional USD 240)
- Hot/cold drinks
- Travel insurance
- Personal expenses
- Tips

- Layered clothing, trekking pants, hiking boots

**Day 5: Namche to Tengboche**

- Overnight: Tengboche (3,956m)

**Day 6: Tengboche to Dingboche**
- Trek: 5-6 hours, 9.2 km
- Overnight: Dingboche (4,410m)

**Day 7: Acclimatization Day in Dingboche**

- Overnight: Dingboche

**Day 8: Dingboche to Lobuche**
- Trek: 5-6 hours, 7 km
- Overnight: Lobuche (4,930m)
- Trail passes through Thukla, memorials of climbers

**Day 9: Lobuche to Gorakshep to Everest Base Camp to Gorakshep**
- Trek: 6-7 hours
- Overnight: Gorakshep (5,164m)

**Day 10: Gorakshep to Kala Patthar to Pheriche**
- Trek: 6-7 hours
- Overnight: Pheriche (4,371m)

**Day 11: Pheriche to Namche**

# GENERAL INFORMATION

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring:** March-May (trekking, blooming rhododendrons)
- **Autumn:** September-November (best season, clear skies)
- **Summer:** June-August (monsoon, risky for trekking)
- **Winter:** December-February (cold, lower altitude treks only)

## Visa Information
- 15 days: USD 30
- 30 days: USD 50
- 90 days: USD 125
- Visa on arrival available for most nationalities

## Daily Budget Recommendations
- **Food:** USD 10-15/day
- **Accommodation (mid-range):** USD 15-30/night
- **Guide/Porter fees:** USD 20-30/day
- **Recommended daily cash:** USD 15-20 for extras
- **Total comfortable budget:** USD 50-70/day

## Extra Costs on Trail
- Hot showers: USD 2-5
- Device charging: USD 2-4
- WiFi: USD 3-5
- Boiled drinking water: USD 1-2
- Soft drinks/beer: USD 2-5
- Tips: guide/porter (expected)

## Key Permits
- **ACAP (Annapurna):** NPR 3,000 (~USD 25)
- **Sagarmatha National Park (Everest):** NPR 3,000
- **Langtang National Park:** NPR 3,000
- **Manaslu Conservation Area Permit:** MCAP + ACAP
- **TIMS Card:** Required for most treks
- **Upper Mustang Restricted Area:** USD 500 (first 10 days)
